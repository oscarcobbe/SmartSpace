Your month

A good month. Your home network did its job, and the two short internet drops were on your provider’s side, not in your house.

The slow evening on 14 September

From 19:00 to 23:00 on Mon 14 Sep, the second floor dropped to between 68 and 99 Mbps, and the first floor access point lost its link three times for a minute or two. Both floors share your electrical wiring, and everything was back to normal by the next morning. That pattern usually means something new was plugged in nearby for the evening, such as a heater or a phone charger. It did nothing worse than slow things down, and a 4K stream needs only 25 Mbps.

If you remember plugging something in that evening, let us know and we will tell you whether it is worth moving. If it happens again we will be in touch first.
